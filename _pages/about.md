### About me <br>

I am a first-year PhD student in Statistics at Columbia University. My research interests lie in machine learning theory, high-dimensional statistics, and information theory.

Before joining Columbia, I completed my master's studies in Mathematics in Paris, France. I spent my first year at Université Paris Cité and my second year at École Normale Supérieure Paris-Saclay, where I pursued the [Master MVA](https://www.master-mva.com/) program. During my studies, I was awarded the Sophie Germain and Smarts-Up Excellence scholarships.

I completed my master's thesis at EPFL under the supervision of [Prof. Florent Krzakala](https://florentkrzakala.com/), studying the complexity of loss landscapes in spiked tensor models. In 2025, I also completed a research internship at Sorbonne University under the supervision of [Prof. Sébastien Martineau](https://martineau-maths.github.io/webpage/index.html), where I studied questions related to stochastic domination in percolation theory. Prior to this, I have collaborated remotely with [Prof. Haohan Wang](https://haohanwang.ischool.illinois.edu/)'s research group at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and [Prof. Min Xu](https://xulabs.github.io/min-xu/)'s group at Carnegie Mellon University.

I earned my B.Sc. in Mathematics and Computer Science from the Sri Aurobindo International Centre of Education in Pondicherry, India.
